## Definition

Describes the widespread, dominant adoption of a practice within a field — often used to mark something as the *default way things are done* rather than a deliberate choice.

## Context

In the prompt-engineering literature, "pervasive" is used to characterise the state of the art as of the early 2020s: hard-coded prompt templates discovered through trial and error are the typical implementation strategy across pipelines, popular frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex), and research practice. The word carries a critical connotation — something is pervasive *and* brittle, pervasive *and* unscalable — framing the status quo as a problem rather than a solved design. The self-learning project's motivation is the pervasiveness of manual prompt iteration for LLM agents.
