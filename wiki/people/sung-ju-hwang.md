## Research areas

Meta-learning, continual learning, transfer learning, and more recently large language model adaptation and prompt optimization. Long history of meta-learning research predating LLM prompt-level applications, which is reflected in the bilevel formulation of [[system-prompt-optimization-meta-learning|MetaSPO]] — meta-learning concepts ported cleanly from model parameters to natural-language prompts.

## Key papers

- [[system-prompt-optimization-meta-learning]] (2025, NeurIPS) — senior author. Introduces bilevel system prompt optimization via meta-learning.

## Recent work

Active in LLM adaptation, prompt optimization, and the application of meta-learning principles to LLM-era problems.
