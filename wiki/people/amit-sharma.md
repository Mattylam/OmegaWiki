## Research areas

Causal inference, machine learning, and more recently LLM prompt optimization and structured reasoning. Long-running interest in robustness, generalization, and structured decomposition of learning problems — visible in [[task-facet-learning-structured-approach-prompt|UniPrompt]] as the structural decomposition of prompt optimization into task facets.

## Key papers

- [[task-facet-learning-structured-approach-prompt]] (2024, ACL Findings 2025) — senior author. Introduces UniPrompt and the task-facet-learning framing of prompt optimization.

## Recent work

Active in LLM prompt optimization, structured reasoning, and the application of causal/structural methods to LLM-era problems.
