## Research areas

LLM programming abstractions, retrieval-augmented generation, prompt optimisation, declarative languages for foundation models. Primary author of the DSPy framework and its predecessors (DSP, ColBERT).

## Key papers

- [[dspy-compiling-declarative-language-model-calls]] (2023) — first author; introduces DSPy programming model with signatures, modules, and teleprompters
- Demonstrate-Search-Predict (DSP) (2022) — predecessor framework that inspired DSPy
- ColBERT, ColBERTv2 — dense retrieval models used in HotPotQA case study

## Recent work

Continues to lead the DSPy project at Stanford NLP; DSPy has become a widely-adopted standard for LLM pipeline programming.
